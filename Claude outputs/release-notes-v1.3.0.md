## 更新内容

- **新增英文界面**：设置 → 语言，可选 跟随系统 / 中文 / English（Android 13 及以上也可以在系统设置的“应用语言”里修改）
- **首页简化**：直播和视频合并为一个输入框，输入直播间号、BV 号或链接，自动判断是直播还是视频
- **登录过期提醒**：B 站登录失效时，开始看弹幕会在悬浮窗里提示重新扫码
- **横竖屏分别记住悬浮窗的位置和大小**
- **修复**：悬浮窗拖到屏幕最上方后与状态栏重叠、无法再拖动的问题；悬浮窗现在不会进入状态栏和底部手势条区域
- 精简了各页面的说明文字

## What's new

- **English UI**: Settings → Language — Follow system / 中文 / English (on Android 13+ you can also change it under "App languages" in system settings)
- **Simpler home screen**: live and video share one input box — enter a room number, BV ID or link and it detects live or video automatically
- **Login expiry reminder**: if your Bilibili login has expired, the overlay tells you to scan the QR code again when you start watching
- **The overlay remembers its position and size separately for portrait and landscape**
- **Fix**: the overlay could get stuck under the status bar after being dragged to the top; it now stays clear of the status bar and the bottom gesture bar
- Trimmed explanatory text across the app
